# WAIS EXECUTION v1.3 — Public Architecture

Updated: 2026-08-12

This document is a sanitized public description of the WAIS execution process. Proprietary model weights, private portfolio data, credentials and internal decision rules must not be stored in the public repository.

## What v1.3 adds

WAIS EXECUTION v1.2 introduced continuous technical analysis from Candidate onward and a TECH READY / Scout Entry stage before READY 1.

WAIS EXECUTION v1.3 keeps the established WAIS research architecture and adds three improvements:

1. **Early Discovery** — technical and market-structure evidence can surface names for research before they become obvious or overextended.
2. **Price Intelligence** — Candidate/Candidate+ and READY 1 states now explicitly separate stock quality from current entry-price quality and timing.
3. **Daily Universe Governance** — every major WAIS status is re-underwritten daily; names can be promoted, downgraded, phased out or removed as evidence changes.

## Execution ladder

1. EARLY DISCOVERY
2. DISCOVERY / RESEARCH
3. CANDIDATE
4. CANDIDATE+
5. TECH READY / SCOUT ENTRY
6. READY 1
7. ADD / HOLD / PROTECT / EXIT
8. PHASE OUT / REJECT

## Early Discovery

Purpose: identify potentially important stocks earlier and route them into WAIS research before price has already moved too far.

Public signal families include:

- Relative-strength improvement
- Price/volume behavior
- Volatility compression / expansion
- Market-structure transition
- Sector / industry rotation

An Early Discovery signal is a **research trigger, not a buy signal**. Fundamental, catalyst, valuation and risk work still has to pass the established WAIS process.

## Candidate and Candidate+

Technical evidence is monitored continuously. WAIS does not wait for READY 1 before reviewing price structure, volume/momentum, moving-average repair/reclaim, relative strength, support, invalidation and event risk.

Candidate-stage review also considers public output fields such as:

- Entry Attractiveness
- Miss Risk / Opportunity Cost
- Support / Invalidation
- Technical Transition Quality
- Event / Liquidity Gate

The purpose is to prevent high-quality names from remaining indefinitely in Candidate/Candidate+ while price moves away.

## TECH READY / Scout Entry

Purpose: permit a deliberately small evidence-based starter position before full READY 1 when the investment case is already strong and technical evidence is beginning to transition.

Public guardrails:

- Price alone cannot create Scout Entry.
- Fundamentals, catalysts and risk/reward must already be acceptable.
- Early transition evidence should be visible across multiple dimensions rather than one isolated indicator.
- The starter tranche remains materially smaller than a normal READY 1 first tranche.
- Invalidation is defined before entry.
- Failed transition returns the name to Candidate / Candidate+; it does not automatically justify averaging down.
- Earnings, macro events, liquidity and extended-hours quality can block or reduce Scout Entry.
- Thin pre-market or after-hours moves are supporting evidence, not equivalent to normal regular-session confirmation.

## READY 1 — Quality, Price and Timing

READY 1 remains the stronger deployment authorization, but v1.3 explicitly separates three public validation dimensions:

- **QUALITY** — is the underlying investment case strong enough?
- **PRICE** — is the current market price still attractive enough?
- **TIMING** — is the current setup suitable for execution now?

A company may meet READY 1-quality standards while the action remains WAIT if price is overextended or risk/reward has deteriorated.

## Daily Universe Governance

WAIS re-underwrites the active universe every day rather than mechanically carrying yesterday's labels forward.

Possible actions:

- PROMOTE
- KEEP
- DOWNGRADE
- PHASE OUT / REMOVE

A broken thesis, vanished catalyst, deteriorating price/risk structure, inferior relative opportunity, or a clearly better replacement can cause a downgrade or removal.

## Operating principle

The objective is not to buy earlier at any cost. The objective is to discover earlier, research faster, improve entry timing, reduce price mismatch and still preserve disciplined evidence-based risk control.

The WAIS core remains an evolving system: improvements are researched, validated and discussed before they are incorporated. Public files show sanitized outputs only; proprietary implementation remains private.
